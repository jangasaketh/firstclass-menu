How a dosa gets here

No packet mix, no shortcuts. It takes about a day to make batter that cooks in 90 seconds.

  1. 1

    Soak

    6 hours

    Ponni rice and urad dal soak separately with a spoon of methi seeds.

  2. 2

    Grind

    40 minutes

    A wet stone grinder turns it slow and cool, so the batter stays airy.

  3. 3

    Ferment

    Overnight

    It rises and turns gently sour. That tang is the whole point.

  4. 4

    Spread

    90 seconds

    One ladle, one spiral on a seasoned cast-iron tawa, a brush of ghee.

Filter coffee, poured from a height.
